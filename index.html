Cognitive Science for Machine Learning

I build AI systems at the intersection of experimental psychology, cognitive science, and applied machine learning. Projects focus on conscientious systems for high-risk domains, and expanding access to specialist mental health insight.

Better Relationships with AI

I want to communicate more meaningfully with AI. I believe that projects can cultivate trust using transparency, and I find closer, collaborative co-creation benefits users and systems.

Research Goals

Emerging cultures often inform widespread advancements. I'm currently researching new high-quality data collection methods in psycho-pharmacological clinical trials, diverse harm reduction, plus how philosophy of mind can help AI address key challenges around sophistication and engagement.
