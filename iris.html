Inferential Robustness Improvement System

Machine Learning Methodology

IRIS stands for Inferential Robustness Improvement System. It brings cognitive scientific principles to AI discovery, empowering innovation from the wider research sector. An elegant framework evaluates AI interactions and decision-making, using occupational scenarios to test for real-world results. We're currently using IRIS to unblock issues in AI Theory of Mind, with more use cases in development.

For example, we measured if an agent's improved inference about another agent's behaviour actually produces better coordination outcomes. Current findings support demonstrations that require new ways to help them cooperate together productively. Research projects at the University of Exeter evaluate potential applications for psychology studies, philosophical disciplines, and humanistic approaches.
